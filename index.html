The care delivery gap

A program is only the beginning.

A referral only helps when someone can enroll, participate and keep receiving support. For small teams, the work between those steps can mean disconnected tools, manual follow-up and records that are difficult to bring together.

Our goal is to make programs easier for providers to operate and easier for people to stay in.

We are prioritizing smaller organizations serving communities with limited access to preventive care, including low-income New Yorkers.

What progress should look like

Measure access, continuity and operational value.

These are the measures we plan to evaluate with provider pilots.

Access
Referral-to-first-session conversion and time to enrollment.
Continuity
Attendance, retention and barriers to continued participation.
Provider capacity
Staff time, documentation completeness and program operating effort.
Financial sustainability
Cost to deliver, participant costs and reimbursement progress where applicable.
